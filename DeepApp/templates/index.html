Deepfake Detection on Social Media: Leveraging Deep Learning and FastText Embeddings for Identifying Machine-Generated Tweets

An advanced artificial intelligence platform designed to combat misinformation across social networks. By extracting semantic subword representations with FastText word embeddings and evaluating syntactic structures with Hybrid Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), this system accurately distinguishes genuine human posts from AI and bot-generated fake content in real-time.

Project Workflow Overview

1. Dataset Ingestion

Parses 25,000+ real & automated tweets from the TweepFake social benchmark.

2. FastText Embeddings

Transforms raw tweets into dense semantic vectors capturing slang, typos, and subwords.

3. Deep CNN & Hybrid Model

Extracts multi-scale spatial patterns and achieves over 92% classification accuracy.

4. Real-Time Detection

Instant classification of any submitted tweet into Human (Normal) or Bot (Fake).
